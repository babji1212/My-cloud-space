What each report shows

  • Class report - average, pass %, highest and lowest, subject bars, toppers and who needs help, for any class and exam.

  • Fees - collected and pending by month and class, and the longest-pending families.
  • Admissions - enquiries to admitted, by month, source and class.
  • Competitive and Year by year - Navodaya/IIT/Olympiad results and a yearly comparison.
